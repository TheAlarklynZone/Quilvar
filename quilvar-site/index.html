ShiftAltV — summon Quick Draw from anywhere

Your clipboard, but it remembers.

Copy three things in a row and normally only the last one survives. Quilvar keeps up to 500 of them, deduplicated and timestamped, searchable the moment you need something you copied an hour — or three clips — ago.

It's local-first: everything lives in a SQLite database on your own machine. Nothing you copy is ever sent anywhere.

Everything in the tray

Eight ways to never lose a clip

Quick Draw

A global hotkey overlay that opens over any app. Type to filter, arrow keys to move, Enter to paste.

Clipboard History

Captures everything you copy automatically, deduplicated, up to 500 clips deep.

Pins

Pin the clips you reuse constantly so they never scroll away, no matter how much you copy after.

Quivers

Group your pinned clips into named collections — one quiver per project, snippet set, or workflow.

Quilvault

A PIN-gated, OS-keychain-encrypted space for the clips you don't want sitting in plain history.

Quilvert

Trim, collapse whitespace, prettify or minify JSON — clean up a clip before you paste it anywhere.

System Tray

Lives quietly in the background. Closing the window sends it to tray — quitting is always intentional.

Auto-updater

One click in Settings checks GitHub Releases and installs the newest build, right down to your channel.
